I built a 50,000-row e-commerce dataset and wrote four SQL questions I actually cared about: where shipping costs were quietly eating margin, which platforms were driving real revenue, how marketing spend compared across channels, and which product categories were actually profitable.

Then I put the answers into one Tableau dashboard, because a spreadsheet full of query results doesn't help anyone make a decision. This is basically what I'd build for your store — one place to see what's working and what's quietly costing you money.
